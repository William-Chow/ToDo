# Privacy Policy for ToDo

Last updated: November 24, 2026

ToDo (shown as "TO-Do" on your phone) is an Android task list published on Google Play by Chow Wee Lim ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept and who can see it.

## In short

- Your tasks are stored on your phone. We don't run any servers, and the app has no accounts or sign-in.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects information about your device and how you use the app, as described below.
- The app does not send your tasks to us or to any advertiser. If Android's own backup is on, Android may include them in the backup kept in your Google account.

## Your tasks

When you add or edit a task, the app saves its title, description, priority, due date, reminder, repeat setting and whether it is done in its private storage on your phone. It also remembers your theme, sort order and selected list. The app does not send any of this anywhere.

## Reminders and permissions

Reminders are scheduled and shown by your phone. A reminder notification shows the task's title and description, so they may be visible on your lock screen, depending on your phone's settings.

The app uses these permissions:

- **Notifications**, to show reminders. If you decline, the rest of the app works as before.
- **Alarms & reminders**, to show a reminder at the time you picked.
- **Run at startup**, to set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
- **Internet, network state, advertising ID and ad services**, for ads. Components the ads rely on also have permission to keep the phone awake and run background work.

You can turn notifications and alarms off at any time in Android settings. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

## Backup

If Android's own backup is turned on, Android may include this app's data, including your tasks, in your phone's backup to your Google account or when you move to a new phone, as it does for other apps. That backup is managed by Google, and we cannot access it. You can turn it off or delete it in your phone's backup settings.

## Advertising

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show a banner ad and an occasional full-screen ad after you save a task. The SDK collects the information below and shares it with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

- device identifiers, such as your advertising ID and app set ID
- your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it
- information about your device and this app, such as the model, operating system and app version
- how you interact with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
- diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage

From version 1.4, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy options** button in the app. Your choice is stored on your phone and sent with ad requests. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in your phone's settings.

Google handles this data under its own policies, and may share it with advertising partners as described in the consent form. See [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What we don't do

- We don't collect, store or receive your tasks or any other personal information on our own servers.
- We don't add analytics or crash-reporting services of our own. The diagnostic information above is collected by Google's ad SDK. Google Play may also show us anonymous crash and usage statistics, depending on your phone's settings.
- We don't sell your information, and we share nothing beyond the ad data described above.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your tasks stay on your phone until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes everything the app has stored on your phone, but not a backup Android keeps in your Google account. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data collected by Google for ads is kept and deleted under Google's policies.

## Security

The app's data is kept in its private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Google states that its ad SDK encrypts the data it sends.

## Children

ToDo is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
